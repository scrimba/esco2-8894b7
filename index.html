The platform

Feed composition sets the process route. The CO₂-only configuration ends after liquid CO₂ recovery. Suitable mixed gas feeds add a methane recovery stage.

Designed for CO₂-bearing exhaust, with pretreatment sized to the actual feed. Industrial liquid CO₂ is the first product target.

Commercial model

A host, an asset and a buyer.

Three agreements must work together at each location. Project capital follows evidence, contracts and offtake, not a generic fleet forecast.

For industrial hosts

Turn a site liability into a qualification project.

ESCo₂ is pursuing financed, operated assets at suitable sites. The structure can reduce host capital burden, but commercial terms, utilities and integration responsibilities must be agreed case by case.

What starts a credible site review

  • Source, location, gas flow and composition
  • Contaminants and variability
  • Power, utilities, land and site rights
  • Storage, transport and local product demand

We screen the feed and market before selecting a configuration or stating a recovery target.
